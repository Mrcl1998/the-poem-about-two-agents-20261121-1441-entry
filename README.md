# The poem about two agents agreeing

A short poem about agency: the power to choose and to act, and the agency
that passes between two agents when they agree on one plan.

`main.py` (pure Python standard library) writes the poem to
`/output/entry.txt`. The poem is 108 characters, inside the 1–280 limit.

## The poem

A plan is a promise
two voices make to one future.
I kept my voice.
You kept yours.
The future kept us both.
